## 5. How far is the stand-in from real bookmaker lines?

On the one real slate we have (AU books, 28 Oct 2026), the stand-in differed from the books' median line by: points MAE 2.1 (only 15% within 0.5), PRA 2.6 (12%), assists 0.8 (36%), rebounds 0.6 (53%), threes 0.3 (67%). Real books clearly use more than a player's recent games, so the ROI figures above are **not** a profit estimate. What carries over: the model's probabilities are roughly calibrated in the 30–70% range, and D1–D4 clearly rank winners above losers; D6 adds a little; D5's result is optimistic (it used who actually sat); D7 and D8 can't be judged without historical game lines and odds.
